**Customer:** Baltic Freight Group (fictional) · Enterprise B2B

### Problems today

- Multi-site quotes are assembled in spreadsheets and re-keyed into the order system.
- Discount approvals run over email, without an audit trail.
- Order errors surface only at provisioning, which delays activation and billing.

### What we will show

- Configure a multi-site offer from the product catalog, with catalog rules enforced
- Price the quote in the opportunity, with discount approval in the same flow
- Convert the accepted quote into an order without re-keying
- Show order decomposition, so fulfilment sees exactly what was sold

### Value of change

A shorter quote-to-order cycle and fewer reworked orders in the B2B segment.

1. One flow from catalog to order: no spreadsheets and no re-keying.
2. Catalog rules stop invalid configurations before the quote, not at provisioning.
3. Fulfilment sees exactly what sales sold, line by line.
